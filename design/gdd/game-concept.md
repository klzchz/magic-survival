# Magical Survive - Game Design Document (v1, 2026-10-06)

**Concept:** co-op survival for up to 4 players, inspired by Don't Starve Together, with magic and mystery in the spirit of Harry Potter and The Name of the Wind, but with an original universe, characters and names (IP-safe).

**Premise:** magic apprentices are trapped in a region where a catastrophe bent the laws of nature. They must build a refuge, master magic and uncover what happened.

## Core mechanics
- **Survival:** health, hunger, temperature, energy, mental stability.
- **Exploration:** living forests, ruins of arcane schools, caves, abandoned villages.
- **Building:** shelters, workshops, gardens, cauldrons, protection circles.
- **Alchemy:** potions from plants, minerals and creature materials.
- **Co-op:** combine abilities; rituals that require several players.
- **Progression:** learn through experimentation, manuscripts and discoveries.

## Magic system
| School | How it works |
|---|---|
| Bindings | Link objects to transfer heat, motion or energy. |
| Inscriptions | Engrave symbols on gear and buildings. |
| Essences | Manipulate properties of plants and minerals via alchemy. |
| Primordial Voices | Discover words that command elements and creatures; rare and dangerous. |

Magic has consequences: heating a shelter can drain heat from your own body; a botched ritual can attract creatures or corrupt the terrain.

## World and goal
Procedural map with day, night and seasons. At night, beings called **Wanderers** (Errantes) hunt sources of magic. Players choose between using their powers or staying hidden.

Goal: restore ancient protection landmarks and stop the corrupted region from spreading.

## MVP
- 1 biome with day/night cycle.
- Co-op up to 4 players.
- Gathering, inventory, crafting, building.
- 6 magic abilities and 5 potions.
- 4 creatures and 1 boss.
- Basic progression and saving.

**Differentiator:** survival depends on understanding and combining the rules of magic, using the environment as a tool.
